⚡ Add a complete song

Just paste the whole song. You do not need to add lines one by one. Put section names on their own line, such as [Intro], [Verse 1], [Pre-Chorus], [Chorus], [Bridge] or [Outro]. Everything underneath a heading automatically belongs to that section until the next heading.

Chord formats supported: chords above lyrics, inline chords like [G]Yesu [C]mere, and simple chord-only lines directly above lyrics.
