⚡ Add a complete song

Just paste the whole song. You do not need to add lines one by one. If the song has section headings such as [Intro], [Verse 1], [Chorus] or [Bridge], the importer will create them automatically. If the source has no headings, that is fine too — you can organize it below using selection or drag-and-drop.

Chords supported: chords above lyrics, inline chords like [G]Yesu [C]mere, and simple chord-only lines above lyrics.
